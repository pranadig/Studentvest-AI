# StudentVest AI

**AI-Powered Investment Guidance for College Students**

StudentVest is an AI-powered investment platform designed to make investing more accessible to college students and first-time investors. It helps users understand investment opportunities, explore personalized stock recommendations, and simulate potential portfolio growth based on their financial goals and risk tolerance.

The project was developed as part of a startup-focused hackathon and earned **Second Place**.

## Project Overview

Many college students are interested in investing but lack the financial knowledge, confidence, or resources to get started. StudentVest aims to simplify that experience by combining financial profiling, AI-powered insights, and portfolio simulations in one platform.

Users provide information about their financial situation, investment budget, goals, time horizon, and risk appetite. The platform uses this information to generate personalized investment insights and help users explore possible portfolio strategies.

## Key Features

- **Financial Profile:** Collects information about the user's budget, expenses, savings, investment amount, goals, and risk tolerance.
- **Personalized Stock Recommendations:** Provides stock suggestions based on the user's financial profile and investment preferences.
- **Portfolio Allocation:** Presents a suggested distribution of investments.
- **Investment Simulations:** Helps users explore potential portfolio growth under different scenarios.
- **Market Insights:** Brings financial information and market news into the investment experience.
- **AI-Powered Explanations:** Makes investment concepts, recommendations, and financial information easier for beginners to understand.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Brings recommendations, portfolio insights, and investment projections together.

## Intended Users

StudentVest is designed primarily for:

- College students exploring investing for the first time
- First-time investors with limited financial knowledge
- Students starting with relatively small investment budgets
- Young investors interested in long-term financial goals
